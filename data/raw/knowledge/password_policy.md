# Password Policy

## Purpose

This policy explains how customers can recover access to their account.

## Allowed

- Provide password reset instructions.
- Explain account recovery steps.
- Direct the customer to the normal login recovery process.

## Restricted

- Do not request the customer's password.
- Do not disclose authentication secrets.
- Do not bypass account security controls.

## Escalation

Escalate the case when the customer reports suspicious account activity.
