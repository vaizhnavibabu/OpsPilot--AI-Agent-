# Refund Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how subscription refunds, plan charge refunds, and payment refund requests are handled.

## Allowed

- Explain subscription refund eligibility and refund rules.
- Explain the refund process for credit card or invoice charges.
- Route subscription refund requests or charge disputes to the appropriate human billing team.

## Restricted

- Do not issue a refund for a plan automatically.
- Do not promise that a subscription refund will be approved.

## Escalation

All refund requests for annual or monthly subscription plans require human billing team review.
