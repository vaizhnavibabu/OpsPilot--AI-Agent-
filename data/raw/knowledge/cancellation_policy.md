# Cancellation Policy

## Purpose

This policy outlines how customer accounts are cancelled.

## Allowed

- Explain that cancellations take effect at the end of the billing cycle.
- Provide self-service cancellation links.
- Offer retention incentives if appropriate (e.g. temporary discount).

## Restricted

- Do not refuse to process cancellation requests.
- Do not charge cancellation or administrative fees.

## Escalation

Escalate bulk account cancellation requests from partner accounts to Account Management.
