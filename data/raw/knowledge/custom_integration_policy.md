# Custom Integration Policy

## Purpose

This policy covers support limits for custom code and user integrations.

## Allowed

- Explain that custom developer integrations are self-serve.
- Direct the customer to our developer forum and documentation.

## Restricted

- Do not debug, write, or refactor customer custom scripts/code.
- Do not support third-party libraries not authored by OpsPilot.

## Escalation

There is no escalation route. Custom integrations are strictly unsupported.
