# Payment Failure Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook helps resolve payment failures and credit card declines.

## Allowed

- Instruct customers to update billing info in settings.
- Suggest retrying the transaction or contacting their card issuer.
- Explain common decline codes (e.g., insufficient funds).

## Restricted

- Do not manually collect credit card numbers in chat/email.
- Do not process payments over unsecured communication channels.

## Escalation

Escalate to billing ops if system-wide gateway errors occur.
