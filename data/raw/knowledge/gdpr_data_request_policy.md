# GDPR Data Request Policy

## Purpose

This policy guides responses to GDPR data rights inquiries.

## Allowed

- Explain the data export and erasure process.
- Provide the link to the Privacy Center to submit formal requests.

## Restricted

- Do not manually delete database records upon informal request.
- Do not share user data without verifying customer identity.

## Escalation

Route formal legal deletion/GDPR notices directly to the Legal & Privacy Officer.
