# Billing Invoice Policy

## Purpose

This policy guides how to handle inquiries about billing invoices.

## Allowed

- Help customers find their invoices in the portal.
- Email copies of paid invoices to the registered billing contact.
- Explain line items on invoices.

## Restricted

- Do not change invoice amounts without manager approval.
- Do not send invoices to unverified email addresses.

## Escalation

Escalate disputes regarding invoice tax calculations or incorrect details.
