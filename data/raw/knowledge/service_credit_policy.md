# Service Credit Policy

## Purpose

This policy handles requests for billing credit due to system downtime.

## Allowed

- Explain the calculation: credits are proportional to downtime duration.
- Inform customers that credits will be applied to the next bill.

## Restricted

- Do not issue credits exceeding one month of subscription fees.
- Do not issue cash or bank-wire refunds for downtime.

## Escalation

Escalate validation of downtime claims to the DevOps and Finance Leads.
