# Abuse Reporting Runbook

## Purpose

This runbook governs reporting of spam, phishing, or system abuse.

## Allowed

- Thank the user for reporting the suspicious behavior.
- Collect headers, URLs, and screenshots of the alleged abuse.

## Restricted

- Do not contact the alleged abuser directly.
- Do not promise immediate suspension of the reported account.

## Escalation

Escalate all abuse cases to the Trust & Safety team.
