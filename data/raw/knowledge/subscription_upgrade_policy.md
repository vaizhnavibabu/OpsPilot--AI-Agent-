# Subscription Upgrade Policy

## Purpose

This policy outlines procedures for customer subscription plan upgrades.

## Allowed

- Detail the features of higher tier plans.
- Explain prorated charging mechanics for upgrades.
- Provide upgrading steps via the customer dashboard.

## Restricted

- Do not apply unauthorized discounts on plan upgrades.
- Do not downgrade plans without customer written consent.

## Escalation

Escalate complex multi-tenant contract pricing requests to sales.
