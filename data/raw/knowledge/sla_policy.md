# SLA Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines our Service Level Agreement response times.

## Allowed

- Quote standard response times: 4 hours for P1, 24 hours for normal.
- Check and communicate ticket priority status.

## Restricted

- Do not guarantee resolution times; only response times.
- Do not promise financial SLA compensation without review.

## Escalation

Escalate breaches of SLA to the Customer Success Director.
